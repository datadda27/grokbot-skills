# Module: Garden (optional)

Version: Prepper v1.2.3 (2026-10-29). Opt-in, off by default.

Sources: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map (https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/), the household's state Cooperative Extension office (the bot finds the official site).

- Ask: in-ground, raised beds, or containers; sun hours; what the family already eats.
- Look up the hardiness zone by ZIP on the USDA map and link the state Extension planting calendar. Planting dates come from Extension, never from the bot.
- A garden never counts toward food days. It shows as "Garden: in season / not in season" because harvest is not a reserve.
- Harvest that is canned joins inventory through `canning.md`.
- Water: the garden plan never uses stored emergency water.
