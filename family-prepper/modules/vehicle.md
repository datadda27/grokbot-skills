# Module: Vehicle add-on (car kit, fuel, EV)

Version: Prepper v1.2.7 (2026-10-30). Opt-in add-on, off by default, never scored. The bot offers it once, only after both kit tiers are started (the stay-home kit and the 72-hour carry kit). It is never part of the mainstream flow, the tiers, or the score. Neutral: no brands pushed. Model details come only from the maker's owner's manual; the bot links the manual and says UNKNOWN otherwise.

## Vehicle kit
- A small kit that lives in the car: water, snacks, a flashlight, first aid, a phone charger, a blanket, and seasonal items (an ice scraper and warm layers in winter, extra water in summer). Ready.gov car safety: https://www.ready.gov/car
- Checked at the April and October check-ins along with the other kits.

## Fuel: the half-tank rule
- Keep at least a half tank of gas at all times, a full tank when evacuation seems likely. (Ready.gov Evacuation: https://www.ready.gov/evacuation)
- This habit is also a standing habit in the method, at every level, even before this module is on. On a tight budget the step is "top off on the next normal trip", not a special trip.

## The car as a charger and short-term shelter
- A running car can charge phones and give a warm or cool place for a short time.
- Carbon monoxide rules (CDC: https://www.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/about/index.html):
  - Never run a car in an attached garage, even with the garage door open.
  - Clear the tailpipe of snow before and while running it.
  - If the tailgate or rear window is open while the car runs, open the vents too.
- A CO alarm belongs at home on every level (the life-safety checks in SKILL.md).

## EV sub-pack (only if the household has an EV)
- **Minimum charge:** keep a sensible charge before a forecast event (an official watch or a storm in the official forecast). The owner can set a floor (for example "never below half"); until they do, do not demand 100% every day.
- **Range:** plan with real range, and less when towing or in the cold. The maker's manual and the car's own estimate are the source.
- **Route chargers and adapters:** note chargers along the evacuation routes and to the out-of-town meeting place, and the adapters the car needs.
- **Climate and power features:** some EVs can hold cabin climate while parked or power devices or a home. For one example maker, see Camp Mode (https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/cybertruck/en_us/GUID-CFA216A5-F2CC-416F-97E8-8560E0CC4161.html) and Powershare (https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/cybertruck/en_us/GUID-E2615021-AB6F-4843-8747-C90D85D94DEA.html). For any other car, use only that maker's manual.
- **Sleeping in the vehicle:** keep fresh air coming in, especially when sleeping in a closed truck bed or cargo area. Follow the manual's ventilation guidance.
