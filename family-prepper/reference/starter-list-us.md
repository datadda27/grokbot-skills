# Two-week starter list (US)

Version: Prepper v1.2.7 (2026-10-30)

A preloaded list for quick-add. The owner says "add the starter list" or ticks lines from it, and the bot scales it to the household with the calculator in `us.md`, then to the prep level. Basics sees about 3 days (roughly a quarter of the 14-day amounts), one weekend's buy, not this whole table. Steady uses the 14-day amounts, still one step per weekend. Serious and All in may look further (a month, then a deep pantry of foods they already eat), still one step, never a dump of the table. Water and the kit items come from Ready.gov and the Red Cross. Food amounts are planning examples, not official figures; say so when you show them, and swap in foods the family already eats.

Rule of thumb for food: buy a little extra of what you already eat each week, put the new item at the back, and eat from the front. No special "survival food" is needed.

## Add when it applies
- Infant: formula, bottles, diapers, wipes, rash cream for 14 days. (Ready.gov Kit)
- Glasses and contact solution. (Ready.gov Kit)
- Medications: work toward 14 days on hand where the prescriber and insurer allow; keep 7 days in the go-bag. (Red Cross) Details stay private.
- Pets: food, water, medicine, leash, carrier, records. (Ready.gov Pets)

## Buy a little each week (budget pacing)
Default pace for a $20 a week budget. The bot rescales to the owner's budget and skips anything already on hand. Prices are rough estimates the bot checks against current local prices before showing a total; it never names a store or brand on its own. Store options follow the shopping rule in SKILL.md (only when the owner asks).

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Life safety first: a CO alarm or smoke alarm if any is missing, then batteries |
| 2 | Water: start with 3 days for everyone |
| 3 | Food: 3 days of foods you already eat, plus a manual can opener |
| 4 | Light: flashlights and spare batteries; test the radio |
| 5 | Water to 1 week |
| 6 | Food to 1 week; sanitation basics (bags, towelettes, bleach) |
| 7 | First aid kit gaps (Red Cross list) |
| 8 | Power banks; document copies |
| 9 to 12 | Water and food to 2 weeks, a little each week |

Never buy during an active shortage or a panic run. If shelves are thin, wait and buy normally later; officials ask for this (for example the UK's 2026 Be Ready campaign said it is "not a call for panic buying").
